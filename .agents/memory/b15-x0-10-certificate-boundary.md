The B15 Core surface may contain only import-free subgroup-product and
no-Frey certificate shapes. The explicit R4(10) rational j-maps, rational
image bridge, 2-adic local conditions, and the Proposition 17/Table 31
valuation-table argument belong solely in the B15 wrapper.

**Why:** The project chose an honest certificate boundary until the cited
valuation tables are fully formalized. B14 remains the sole factorization
boundary, so B15 must not reintroduce a radical or prime-factorization
implementation.

**How to apply:** When the valuation cases are formalized, prove the existing
wrapper-level table certificate and feed it through the wrapper theorem to
obtain the Core no-Frey certificate. Do not place rational functions,
padic valuations, or source-dependent classification claims in Core.
